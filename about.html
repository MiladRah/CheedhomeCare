About us

Care for elders is a way of life for us. Here's what sets us apart.

CHEED Inc. was founded by individuals raised in traditions where caring for elders is a way of life.

In our cultures, elders are deeply respected. They are seen as the heart of the family, carrying wisdom, experience, love and guidance. Many of us grew up with our grandparents living with us or nearby. Caring for them, checking on them, preparing meals, spending time with them, and making sure they felt included was a natural part of daily life.

As families settle into busy lives, many seniors still want to remain in their own homes, surrounded by comfort, routine and independence. At the same time, families may need extra support to make sure their loved ones are safe, cared for, and not left alone. CHEED Inc. was created to help meet that need with compassion, reliability and respect.

What we do

At CHEED Inc., we provide non-clinical home support services that help seniors and families with daily living.

Our services include companionship, light housekeeping, meal preparation, grocery shopping, errands, transportation to appointments, medication reminders, respite support for family caregivers, and personal care support by trained staff where appropriate.

What makes us different

What makes CHEED different is the values behind our care.

We believe that supporting seniors is not only about completing tasks. It is about listening with patience, treating each person with dignity, respecting their routines, and helping them feel valued in their own home. We understand that inviting someone into your home requires trust, and we take that responsibility seriously.

For us, this work is personal. It is about honouring the people who came before us and helping families feel confident that their loved ones are supported, respected, and cared for at home.

CHEED Inc. does not provide nursing care, clinical care, medication administration, or medical treatment.

Our mission is to honour seniors with compassionate, dependable care rooted in trust and cultural understanding, so families feel confident their loved ones are aging with dignity and comfort, right at home.
