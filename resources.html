Resources for seniors in Alberta

Navigating government programs can be complex, so we've gathered key Government of Alberta programs that can help elders living independently or looking for extra support.

Financial assistance

  • Alberta Seniors Benefit

    A monthly benefit for eligible seniors with low income, to help with living expenses.

    Learn more about Alberta Seniors Benefit (opens in a new tab)

  • Special Needs Assistance for Seniors

    Financial help for eligible seniors with low income toward some appliances and specific health and personal supports.

    Learn more about Special Needs Assistance for Seniors (opens in a new tab)

  • Supplementary Accommodation Benefit

    Financial help for eligible seniors with low income who live in a continuing care home.

    Learn more about Supplementary Accommodation Benefit (opens in a new tab)

  • Seniors Home Adaptation and Repair Program

    A low-interest home equity loan to help senior homeowners repair, adapt or renovate so they can stay safely at home.

    Learn more about Seniors Home Adaptation and Repair Program (opens in a new tab)

Health coverage

  • Coverage for Seniors

    Alberta Blue Cross coverage that helps with costs such as prescription drugs, ambulance services and home nursing care.

    Learn more about Coverage for Seniors (opens in a new tab)

  • Dental and Optical Assistance for Seniors

    Help with the cost of basic dental and optical services for eligible seniors with low to moderate income.

    Learn more about Dental and Optical Assistance for Seniors (opens in a new tab)

Help and information

  • Apply online

    One online application enrols seniors in the Alberta Seniors Benefit, Special Needs Assistance, Dental and Optical Assistance, and Coverage for Seniors programs.

    Go to application about Apply online (opens in a new tab)

  • 211 Alberta

    A free, confidential helpline and website that connects you with community, social and government services near you. Call or text 211.

    Visit 211 Alberta about 211 Alberta (opens in a new tab)

  • Health Link 811

    Free 24/7 health advice from a registered nurse. Call 811 from anywhere in Alberta.

    About Health Link about Health Link 811 (opens in a new tab)

Program details and eligibility change from time to time. Please check the official government pages for the latest information. Need a hand with forms? Helping with paperwork is one of our services.
